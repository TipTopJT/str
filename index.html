About StructiveAI

Technology built on research. Solutions built for reality.

StructiveAI is a research-driven engineering and technology company working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, information technology, automation, electronics and mechanical design.

We conduct applied research, develop new algorithms and intellectual property, and create solutions that improve the efficiency, reliability and performance of industrial, technological and organizational processes.

Our work covers the full development lifecycle—from the initial concept and digital design to prototyping, software and hardware integration, production setup and implementation.
